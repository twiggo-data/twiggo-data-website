How I can help

My numbers don’t match

If your booking system, tills or spreadsheets give you different totals, I’ll trace the figures back to their source, help you agree which ones to use and put simple checks in place so you can spot problems early.

I don’t know where to start

I’ll help you choose what to track first and build a simple report from the records you already keep.

I want to use AI but don’t know how

I’ll show you where AI tools can save time in your business, such as summarising guest reviews, drafting product descriptions or answering questions about your sales, and where they’ll confidently give you the wrong answer. Then I’ll help get your data in shape so the answers you get are ones you can trust.

My team needs to get more from our data

Practical, hands-on training for you and your team, built around your own reports and systems: how to read the numbers, how to get more out of Excel or Power BI, and how to use AI tools safely with customer data.

About Twiggo Data

Meet Kate Sherrard

I started out on the other side of the data. I spent the first eight years of my career in retail merchandising, deciding what to buy, how to price it and when to promote it. I ordered stock, planned ranges and ran the gold hedging process. I was also the person the team came to when the numbers didn’t add up, so I know what it’s like to make a decision with figures you don’t trust.

For more than a decade since then, I’ve worked in hotel data. I’ve helped a national hotel chain understand its bookings, pricing and performance, and I’ve led the teams that build the reports managers check every morning. Most recently I fixed how the booking figures behind those reports arrive: they’re now complete and on time on 99% of days, up from about 80%, and they land four hours earlier.

Your business will be smaller, but the problems are usually the same: two systems that give different totals, a spreadsheet nobody quite trusts, or a report that takes half of Monday to pull together. I’ve spent my career sorting out exactly those.

I taught myself every tool I use, from Excel and SQL to Power BI. Whatever you run on, whether that’s a booking system, tills or a stack of spreadsheets, I’ll learn it.

And yes, the dachshund is real. Her name is Twiggy, and she’s the reason this business has a name.
