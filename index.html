About Twiggo Data

Meet Kate Sherrard

I started out on the other side of the data. I spent the first part of my career in retail merchandising, deciding what to buy, how to price it and when to promote it. I ordered stock, planned products and ran the gold hedging process. I was also the person the team came to when the numbers didn’t add up, so I know what it’s like to make a decision with data you don’t trust.

Since then I’ve spent more than a decade in hospitality data, working my way up through commercial analytics and business intelligence to analytics engineering, leading teams at each stage. I built my employer’s first analytics engineering function, moved teams onto modern data platforms, and rebuilt a business-critical reservations pipeline that went from around 80% reliable to 99% and delivers four hours earlier. In 2026 I presented that work at Big Data London.

I taught myself every tool I use, from SQL and Python to Power BI and Databricks. I’ll learn your systems too.

Twiggo Data exists for businesses that need their data to just work. You don’t need an enterprise platform or a team of consultants. You need data products built on the right foundations, so your figures mean the same thing every time anyone looks at them.

And yes, the dachshund is real. Her name is Twiggy, and she’s the reason this business has a name.
