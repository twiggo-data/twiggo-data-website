How I can help

My numbers don’t match

If your systems or spreadsheets give you different totals, I’ll trace the figures back to their source, help you agree which ones to use and put simple checks in place so you can spot problems early.

I don’t know where to start

I’ll help you choose what to track first and build a simple report from the records you already keep.

I want to use AI but don’t know how

I’ll show you where AI tools can save time in your business, such as summarising customer feedback, drafting product descriptions or answering questions about your sales, and where they’ll confidently give you the wrong answer. Then I’ll help get your data in shape so the answers you get are ones you can trust.

My team needs to get more from our data

Practical, hands-on training for you and your team, built around your own reports and systems: how to read the numbers, how to get more out of Excel or Power BI, and how to use AI tools safely with customer data.

About Twiggo Data

Meet Kate Sherrard

Most small businesses don’t have a data team. They have a booking system, a till, a few spreadsheets, and figures that never quite agree.

Twiggo Data helps you make sense of the data you already have. I find out why your numbers don’t match, build simple reports you can trust, and show you where AI can genuinely save you time, and where it’ll confidently give you the wrong answer.

How I can help

  • My numbers don’t match: I’ll trace the differences back to where they start and help you agree which figures to use
  • I don’t know where to start: I’ll help you choose what to track first and build a report from the records you already keep
  • I want to use AI but don’t know how: practical advice on where AI fits in your business, and how to use it safely with customer data
  • My team needs to get more from our data: hands-on training built around your own reports and systems

I’m Kate Sherrard. I started in retail merchandising and have spent more than a decade in data since, leading teams and building the reports large businesses run on. I taught myself every tool I use, and I’ll learn yours too.
